About

I am a second-year Master’s student in Applied Mathematics at Western University, supervised by David Jeffrey and Chris Kapulkin.

My research interests lie at the intersection of algebraic topology, combinatorics, and computation. I am particularly interested in using topological data analysis to study directed and higher-order data.

In my free time I enjoy running and playing/watching sports.
