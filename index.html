About

Mathematics for complex structure and data

I am a second-year Master’s student in Applied Mathematics at Western University, supervised by David Jeffrey and Chris Kapulkin.

My research interests lie at the intersection of algebraic topology, combinatorics, and computation. I am particularly interested in using topological data analysis to study directed, higher-order, and hypergraph-based data.

Research

Current interests

I am interested in mathematical tools that retain structure often lost in ordinary graph and metric-space models.

01

Topological Data Analysis

Persistent and applied homological methods for extracting robust qualitative structure from complex data.

02

Hypergraphs

Higher-order network models in which relationships can involve more than two vertices at a time.

03

Computational Topology

Algorithms and implementations for computing topological invariants of combinatorial and network-based structures.
